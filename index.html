THE PRESENT AGE

A time of diminished dharma

Kali Yuga is the fourth and shortest age in the traditional four-yuga cycle. It is associated with conflict, moral decline, weakened dharma, and social disorder. This reckoning belongs to Hindu cosmology and is presented here as a traditional chronology, not as a scientific prediction.

Passages on Kali Yuga

“Property alone will confer rank.”

— Vishnu Purana, Book IV, Chapter XXIV
About the reckoning ↓

The traditional duration of Kali Yuga is 432,000 human years, with the commonly used epoch beginning in 3102 BCE. Adding the full duration without a BCE/CE year zero produces an ending year of 428,899 CE.

The exact day and second displayed by this website are a modern implementation convention rather than a universally authoritative scriptural timestamp.
